    FAQ

    How do I get there?+

    Take the train from Cais do Sodré to Alcântara-Mar (6 minutes) or trams 15E and 18E. There is no public parking on site, so we recommend public transport.

    Can I leave and come back in?+

    Yes. Your wristband lets you go in and out as many times as you like on the days you bought.

    Is there food for vegetarians and vegans?+

    Yes. Every food truck has at least one vegetarian option, and there is a fully vegan area next to the River Stage.

    What is the age limit?+

    Anyone under 16 must be with an adult. Children under 6 enter for free.

    Can I get a refund?+

    Tickets can be refunded until 30 days before the festival. After that, you can transfer your ticket to a friend for free in your account.
